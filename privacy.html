Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains how FEFCO Solutions (FEFCO Electro Mechanical Services LLC, Dubai, United Arab Emirates) collects and uses the details you share with us when you fill in one of our forms, call us, message us or book a visit.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number and email address.
  • The area or community of your property and the service you are interested in.
  • Your preferred date and time for a visit or call, and anything you tell us about your requirement.
  • If you call us: the number you call from, the time of the call and, when our virtual assistant answers, a recording and written summary of the call.
  • If you reach us through a Facebook or Instagram ad: the ad and form you responded to.

How we use it

  • To contact you about your enquiry, give you a quote and book a site visit or phone consultation.
  • To send you confirmations and reminders about your booking by WhatsApp, SMS, email or phone.
  • To keep a record of your enquiry and our service history with you.
  • To understand which of our adverts work, in summary form.

We do not sell your details and we do not use them for unrelated marketing without your consent.

Who we share it with

Only with the service providers that help us run our business, and only as needed: our customer-records and booking systems, our calendar and email providers, our telephone and virtual-assistant provider, and Meta (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp) when you contact us through their platforms. These providers process your details on our behalf. We may also share information when required by UAE law.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and booking details for as long as needed to serve you and to meet our legal and accounting obligations, usually no longer than 3 years after our last contact with you. Call recordings are kept for up to 12 months.

Your choices

  • You can ask us at any time for a copy of your details, to correct them or to delete them.
  • You can stop WhatsApp messages by replying STOP, and stop reminder calls by telling us.

Security

Your details are stored in access-controlled systems and are only available to FEFCO staff who need them to serve you.
